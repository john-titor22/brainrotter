# Privacy Policy — Brainrotter

Brainrotter is a personal, non-commercial project. It does not collect,
store, sell, or share personal data belonging to any third party.

- The app runs locally on the developer's own machine.
- The only accounts it accesses are the developer's own social media
  accounts, authorized directly by the developer via each platform's
  standard OAuth flow.
- Access tokens are stored locally on the developer's machine and are
  never transmitted to any third party.
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising services are used.

Contact: see the repository at
https://github.com/john-titor22/brainrotter for more information.
